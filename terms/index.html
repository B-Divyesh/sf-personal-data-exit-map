Policy sheet / T-01

Terms

Effective 28 August 2026

A preservation aid, not a transfer guarantee

Personal Data Exit Map inventories export packages and suggests preservation steps. It does not log into services, import data, restore accounts, guarantee completeness, or preserve legal access rights, licenses, subscriptions, live social relationships, or service functionality.

Check important results

Detection is based mainly on file names, paths, sizes, and formats. Service exports change. Labels can be incomplete or wrong, and encrypted file contents are not inspected. Verify representative files and follow the parser support notes before deleting an account or original archive.

Local signatures

A signed manifest proves that a manifest was signed by the key generated in that browser. The SHA-256 identifies the selected archive at inspection time. This is preservation evidence, not identity verification, a trusted timestamp, a legal certification, or proof that an export is complete.

Acceptable use

Use the utility only with exports you are authorized to possess. Do not use it to bypass access controls, collect another person’s private information, or violate a service’s terms or applicable law.

No warranty

The software is provided under the MIT License, “as is”, without warranty. Keep the original export, maintain more than one copy, and seek professional advice when legal retention or regulated records are involved.
